Lights Out

You Win!

How to Play

Click on tiles to toggle them and adjacent tiles on/off. Turn off all lights to win!

Addendum:

The objective of the game is to light out all the boxes on the board. When all boxes are lighted out, a message box will display "You Win!"

The game features a 5x5 grid where some boxes are initially colored at random.

Players must strategically click on the boxes and turn off all the on lights.

The "New Game" button resets the board to its original state, allowing players to start a fresh game. The "Help" button opens a dialog with instructions and a close option.

The game recognizes a win when every box is turned off. Players receive instant feedback and gratification upon completing the game.
